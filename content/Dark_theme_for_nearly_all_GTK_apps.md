## Installation

to install it on Debian:
`sudo apt install nwg-look -y`
in Arch it's something like `nwg-look-bin`, just search

## How to use it ?

just run in the terminal `nwg-look`, & choose the wedgets & the icons theme, etc

click **Apply**, & then click **Close**.
